Sunday, November 8, 2026 · Weekend edition

Walleye to return about $2bn; Arini's flagship slides to -32% as an ADIA unit seeks $7.5bn for private markets

Top stories
Launch

Walleye plans to return about $2bn and slim its hedge fund book to roughly $10bn

Friday's Bloomberg follow-up names the figure that Wednesday's letter left open. Dockside SMAs keep about $5bn more, and the firm is leaning into vol, quant and external managers after a 5.9% October. LPs will want timing, mechanics and who stays.

Investing.com, citing Bloomberg →
Performance

Arini's master fund is down 32% this year after a 19.7% October

That is a further slide from the roughly 26% year-to-date figure circulating in late October. The firm cut gross leverage to its lowest since early 2022 and re-underwrote its five largest positions, yet still took $389m of inflows last week as part of a $1.5bn raise. Conviction plus fundraising through a drawdown is the IR test.

Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg →
Gulf

An ADIA subsidiary launches a $7.5bn loan to fund private-market investments

The secured subscription line, reported Friday via LPC, is meant for existing and future infrastructure, private equity and real estate deals. It has a one-year tenor plus two one-year extension options, with commitments due 27 November and signing slated for 11 December. FAB, HSBC, Societe Generale and Standard Chartered are arranging it. A reminder that Abu Dhabi's largest sovereign is still financing private-market deployment into ADFW season.

Al Khaleej, citing LPC →
Rules

Man Group faces about $155m after a London judge sided with Kuwait's state pension

The High Court ruled on Thursday that two subsidiaries are vicariously liable because two former regional sales staff knew an intermediary was paying Fahad al Rajaan at PIFSS. Shares fell as much as 5.9% on Friday. The dollar figure is not final (a mid-November hearing will settle deductions, interest and costs), and Man says it is a 1993 legacy contract it no longer markets. Gulf LPs will ask how you diligence intermediaries.

Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg →

LaunchLaunches, raises & closures

New funds, capital raised, fund closes, wind-downs and fundraising halts.

  • Walleye plans to return about $2bn after cutting back internal equities, which would take hedge fund assets to about $10bn, matching the start of the year, while Dockside SMAs keep about $5bn more (Investing.com, citing Bloomberg; Nishant Kumar).
  • Arini still raised money through the drawdown, taking $389m of inflows over the past week as part of a broader $1.5bn fundraising effort (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • XN founder Gaurav Kapadia told investors in a Thursday letter that the firm intends to be a "large participant" buying more Anthropic shares at the planned IPO, after a stake valued at about $5.3bn at May's $965bn round (Hedgeweek, citing the FT).

LPsAllocators & mandates

Pensions, endowments, insurers, SWFs, family offices and FoFs: commitments, results, surveys and policy shifts.

  • Kuwait's Public Institution for Social Security won a London High Court ruling on Thursday in its long-running bribery case, with the judgment indicating it could recover about $155m from Man Group subsidiaries and other defendants, subject to a further mid-November hearing (Man Group RNS, 5 Nov).
  • Virginia Retirement System committed €125m ($139.6m) to Igneo European Diversified Infrastructure Fund IV, which closed at its €6bn hard cap after launching only six months earlier and drawing pensions, insurers, sovereigns and family offices above a €4bn target (Institutional Real Estate, 6 Nov).

GulfGulf capital & Abu Dhabi

ADIA, Mubadala, ADQ/L'IMAD, PIF, QIA and peers, plus managers setting up in ADGM and DIFC.

  • An ADIA-owned subsidiary has launched a $7.5bn secured subscription-line facility for private-market investments in infrastructure, private equity and real estate, with commitments due 27 November and signing planned for 11 December. First Abu Dhabi Bank, HSBC, Societe Generale and Standard Chartered are mandated arrangers, and Standard Chartered is sole coordinator (Al Khaleej, 6 Nov, citing LPC; English write-up: Arabian Post).
  • Abu Dhabi property transactions jumped 76% to Dh171.26bn ($46.6bn) in the first nine months of 2026, and foreign investment in the emirate's investment zones rose 131% to Dh86.11bn, the Abu Dhabi Real Estate Centre said on Saturday (The National).
  • Bloomberg argues that the Iran war has exposed two further Gulf vulnerabilities beyond oil dependence, and made all three harder to solve, useful context for anyone planning roadshows into year-end (Bloomberg via X).

PerfPerformance & flows

Index and peer returns, notable fund numbers, redemptions and prime-book positioning.

  • Arini's flagship lost 19.7% in October, taking the year-to-date decline to 32%, and the firm said it had underestimated dislocation in CCC-rated debt (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • Said Haidar's Jupiter macro fund fell 11.5% in October, its third double-digit monthly drop of 2026, leaving it up 6.3% for the year. Assets were $702m at the end of September, down from a peak of around $5bn in 2022 (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • Institutional Investor says Sosin Partners, with nearly 80% of the book in Carvana, was down 24.12% through September and on track for its first loss in four years (II, paywalled; II on X).
  • Friday write-ups of October long/short marks that earlier editions did not carry: Tiger Global's flagship -8.5% (roughly flat for 2026), Whale Rock about -8% (still up about 34% year to date) and D1's equity book -4.4% (18.8% year to date) (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).

TalentPlatforms, PM moves & seeding

Multi-manager hiring, pod builds, spinouts, seed and acceleration deals.

  • Two more credit pods are leaving Millennium: Dominick Maggio and Amit Agrawal of Abacove Capital, and Mark Meenan and David Ulrich of Twin Lights, according to people familiar. Reasons were not disclosed. Millennium was down 2.3% in October, trimming year-to-date gains to 5.7% (Hedgeweek; Nishant Kumar).
  • Former Jain Global portfolio manager Mike Scheer has joined Walleye to work directly for CIO Will England, as the firm keeps a slimmer internal stock-picking effort beside external managers (Investing.com, citing Bloomberg).

TermsFees, terms & structures

Fee levels, pass-through, liquidity terms, share classes, evergreen and semi-liquid wrappers.

  • Walleye's return would cut the hedge fund book to about $10bn while leaving the Dockside SMA platform at about $5bn, a concrete answer to the "optimal fund size" question posed in Wednesday's letter (Investing.com, citing Bloomberg).
  • Arini cut gross leverage to its lowest level since early 2022 and says it will diversify more, while still taking in new capital, a combination LPs will want explained in the next update (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).

RulesMarketing, ops & compliance

AIFMD II, SEC marketing rule, Form PF, FINRA, FCA, ADGM FSRA/DFSA and enforcement touching how funds market.

  • Man Group's 5 Nov RNS says two former regional sales employees knew intermediary Mohamad El Ghazzi was making improper payments to PIFSS's late director general, making two subsidiaries vicariously liable. The firm disputes liability and quantum, notes there were no negative findings on the broader firm, and is evaluating its options, including a possible appeal (Man Group RNS).
  • Judge Richard Jacobs criticised an internal compliance review, run when current CEO Robyn Grew was global head of compliance, for confirmation bias after it cleared those involved and let the intermediary relationship continue. Grew herself was not directly criticised. The takeaway for IR is that LPs may ask about review culture, not only the dollar figure (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg; Insurance Journal).
