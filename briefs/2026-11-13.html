LPs

CPPIB plans to double its hedge fund allocation to about $160bn

The C$866bn Canadian pension has about $80bn with more than 60 hedge funds today (Two Sigma, Bridgewater, ExodusPoint, Rokos, Magnetar among them) and wants roughly double that over five to seven years, mixing big multistrategy platforms with specialists; Millennium is among managers it is considering. External hedge funds drove much of the $3.4bn its Capital Markets and Factor Investing unit earned in the year to March 2026. The single biggest new demand signal for the industry this year, and a reason to have a Toronto plan for 2027. Bloomberg, unnamed sources, reported late 11 November.

New Mexico PERA considers eliminating its hedge fund allocation

The pension has historically held a 6% target to absolute return and is reviewing it as part of a broader asset allocation reset, after a reported $100m redemption from Two Sigma in September. No decision yet, and it has not said where the money would go. The same day Colorado FPPA committed $240m to Millennium and NovaCore, so the US public pension market is splitting: IR teams should expect more "justify the diversifier" questions from boards. P&I reporting, via Hedgeweek and Opalesque.

Gulf

Blackstone opens a DIFC office as its Gulf private wealth hub

It is Blackstone's second UAE office after Abu Dhabi, where it has been since 2010 and has just upgraded its licence for deal origination. The Dubai team builds on more than 40 distributor relationships across the region, with a Kuwait office planned and further GCC expansion expected over the coming year. Wealth distribution through Gulf private banks is now a contested channel, which matters for any manager hoping to raise from Gulf HNW and family office money alongside the sovereigns.

Rules

FSB to consult within weeks on data for hedge funds' leveraged bond bets

The Financial Stability Board's Non-Bank Data Task Force, chaired by Andrew Bailey, missed its mid-2026 report target on the basis trade and will instead launch a public consultation on metrics that are meaningful but achievable; FSB consultations usually run 60 days, pushing this into 2027. Relative value and macro managers running Treasury basis trades should expect more data asks, both from regulators and from allocators doing their own leverage diligence. Bloomberg, people familiar.
