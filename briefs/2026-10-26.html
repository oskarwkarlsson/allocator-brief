Weil's incoming chair Ramona Nee turns down the job and has held talks with Simpson Thacher

Nee advises big PE sponsors including Advent, Weil's merger talks with Willkie ended last week, and rivals are recruiting its partners. Funds that use Weil as counsel should expect ODD and LP questions about adviser continuity, so have an answer ready.

ADGM's new Institutional Fund Manager route has no AUM cap and lighter capital rules

Managers running only funds with a $5m minimum and no individual investors skip the finance officer, internal audit and indemnity insurance rules and hold six weeks of expenses as capital instead of 13. Open-ended funds qualify, so it suits hedge fund platforms serving sovereign money. The rules date from Sept 16; this is a fresh law-firm read (Oct 26).
