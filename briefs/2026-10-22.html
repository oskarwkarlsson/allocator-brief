New funds, capital raised, fund closes, wind-downs and fundraising halts.

LPsAllocators & mandates

PerfPerformance & flows

Index and peer returns, notable fund numbers, redemptions and prime-book positioning.

TermsFees, terms & structures

Fee levels, pass-through, liquidity terms, share classes, evergreen and semi-liquid wrappers.

  • The SEC's Sept 30 proposal would let more registered funds and BDCs charge performance fees (capped at 20%, on realised or unrealised gains) and broaden the qualified client standard. Law firm notes landed this week. JD Supra
  • The SEC also proposes modernising interval funds and widening multiple share classes for closed-end funds, both relevant to retail and semi-liquid alts wrappers. JD Supra
