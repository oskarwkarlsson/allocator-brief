Two Sigma explores selling a minority stake, with a Middle Eastern institution and a US pension in early talks

The $85bn quant would add a third shareholder to break deadlocks between Overdeck and Siegel, who each own about half; it favours an existing LP and a deal could take 12 to 18 months. For IR teams this is the GP-stakes-for-governance playbook, and a reminder that LPs now buy into managers, not just funds. Single source (Bloomberg).

Schwarzman: Blackstone has committed more to the Gulf in the past year than ever, and plans a 3x to 4x bigger regional book by 2030

He says the Gulf is turning from pure capital exporter into a place with its own financing needs, so global managers are pivoting from raising there to investing there. Expect allocators like ADIA and Mubadala to get pitched co-invest and local deals alongside fund commitments.
