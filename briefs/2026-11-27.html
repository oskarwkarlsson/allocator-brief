Situational Awareness lost 65% after fees in Q3, yet is still up 88.5% for the year

Margin calls on leveraged AI bets nearly sank the fund in July, and it sold much of its public book to Citadel. Aschenbrenner’s letter promises more measured risk, with the book now about 70% public and 30% private. A live case study in why allocators ask about leverage and prime concentration, not just returns. WSJ, read via write-ups.

LaunchLaunches, raises & closures

New funds, capital raised, fund closes, wind-downs and fundraising halts.

  • InCred Global Asset Management launched its first EM credit hedge fund, the InCred EM Multi-Strategy Credit Hedge Fund, a trading-oriented long/short with an Asian bias that aims to reach $1bn over five years, with India gross exposure mostly under 10% (Financial Express). The portfolio includes West Asia, so expect it in Gulf allocator pipelines.
  • QRT background: spun out of Credit Suisse in 2018 with about $800m, it is now a $60bn multistrategy firm and keeps consolidating vehicles (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • From 25 Nov: Nexent Capital launched Nexent Capital ADGM Fund I, a closed-end AI infrastructure and advanced energy vehicle seeking $1bn with a $1.6bn hard cap (FundOpsHQ, single source).

LPsAllocators & mandates

Pensions, endowments, insurers, SWFs, family offices and FoFs: commitments, results, surveys and policy shifts.

  • JPMorgan AM (with DBS), BlackRock and Endowus (talking to Capital Group) lead the race to run Singapore CPF’s new voluntary lifecycle scheme for the S$701bn fund; winners in H1 2027, launch in 2028 (Bloomberg, Asean Coverage; people familiar).
  • From 24 Nov: New York State Common ($310bn) is weighing a roughly $4bn collateralised fund obligation backed by private credit fund stakes, including Blackstone and Blue Owl funds (Bloomberg, people familiar). A big pension engineering liquidity out of illiquids is a signal for how stretched alternatives budgets are.
  • The IMF flagged emerging risks in Australia’s super system, including keeping enough assets liquid as members retire (Bloomberg).
  • From 11 Nov: Korea’s POBA is picking up to five managers for a $150m hedge fund mandate, covering domestic equity strategies and overseas fund of hedge funds; proposals closed 25 Nov (KED Global, POBA notice).
