LPs

CPP Investments folds its losing stockpickers into a new Active Public Markets unit

Canada's C$866.2bn pension merged Active Equities into its Capital Markets and Factor Investing group under Heather Tobin, a unit that runs internal teams and "trusted external partners" as one portfolio. Active Equities head Frank Ieraci steps back; the Globe and Mail reports dozens of layoffs after the unit lost C$3.5bn in fiscal 2026. Coming a day after APG quit active equities, it is another big allocator saying liquid active risk must earn its fees, and a door opening for external hedge fund and systematic partners.

Rokos loses 4.9% in October as Renaissance gains 6%

Rokos (about $22bn) is now up about 8% for the year, joining a list of big names in the red alongside Viking (-1.2%), while Renaissance's Institutional Equities fund rose 6% (12.3% YTD) and Institutional Diversified Alpha 4.6% (22.4% YTD). Expect allocators to benchmark every October letter against this list. Bloomberg reporting, via Hedgeweek and Opalesque.

Indiana's $55bn pension accuses Jefferies of betrayal over its Leucadia hedge fund

The Indiana Public Retirement System says Jefferies courted it intensively, including hosting senior staff, before its investment in 352 Capital, run by Jefferies' Leucadia Asset Management arm. A sharp reminder for IR teams that the courtship record can resurface when a relationship sours. Single-source (Bloomberg, via Opalesque).

Mubadala Capital takes a minority stake in $5bn secondaries manager LSV Advisors

LSV runs GP-led, single-asset continuation vehicles and keeps its leadership and strategy; closing is expected in the first half of 2027. Mubadala Capital keeps assembling a multi-strategy platform, weeks after Apollo agreed to buy into it with about $5bn of strategic commitments, so expect more Abu Dhabi-anchored product to land on wealth and institutional shelves.
