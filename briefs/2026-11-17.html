DIFC held 102 hedge fund managers at end-October, flat on the year despite the war

DIFC figures shown at a Dubai briefing: 102 managers, more than 80% running over $1bn, double the 50 at the start of 2024, with two-thirds from the US and UK. The DFSA says firms now send “risk takers” and senior decision-makers rather than back offices, and LGT's Middle East CEO reports strong local interest in market-neutral strategies. Growth has paused but nobody left, and Gulf private banks want low-volatility products: worth knowing before any Gulf marketing trip.

Mubadala and Centerbridge agree to buy Canaccord Wealth at a £1.17bn enterprise value

The all-cash deal covers Canaccord Genuity's UK and Crown Dependencies wealth arm, with £43.4bn in assets under management and administration; management keeps a “meaningful” stake and closing is expected in the first half of 2027. Mubadala's Yasir Badri cites growing demand for advice. Abu Dhabi capital is buying distribution into private wealth, the same channel many managers are now trying to reach.

Q3 13Fs arrive as the Nasdaq drops 2.49% into Nvidia week

Monday's filings show D1 adding heavily to Lattice and Qnity, Coatue adding to two megacaps, Tiger Global lifting Cerebras 48% to $789m and cutting Alphabet 42%, and Pershing Square at $30.8bn in 16 holdings. The same day the Nasdaq fell 2.49% for a third straight loss, with Nvidia down 4.1%. LPs are reading 30 September books in a very different tape, so be ready to explain what has changed since quarter end.
