Millennium pulls more than $1.5bn from two external managers

Millennium is redeeming over $1bn of leveraged capital from event-driven Troluce Capital and about $500m from Jack Land's Axebrook Capital, both backed last year, after trading losses. Platform tickets can build a young manager fast, and leave just as fast: IR teams relying on them need a diversified LP base behind the headline allocation.

ADIA, Avolon and I Squared launch Constellation Aviation

A new aircraft leasing and infrastructure platform seeded with a $1.4bn portfolio of 22 aircraft on lease to 17 airlines, with Avolon as minority owner and exclusive servicer. Another sign that ADIA's infrastructure team prefers co-owned platforms with operating partners over plain fund commitments.

PivotalPath: the ten biggest multistrats run $2.2tn with about 6x leverage

The ten largest multistrategy funds held $2.2tn including borrowed money at end-2025 against $360bn of net assets, with pod shops at 8 to 14 times, and their US stock holdings overlap more with each other than with other hedge funds. Expect allocators to ask harder questions about crowding and correlation to the platforms.

EQT in talks with UAE and Saudi sovereign investors on AI co-investment

Chair Jean Eric Salata says EQT ($400bn AUM) is discussing joint AI and data centre deals with Gulf sovereigns, after decades of raising money there; it opened an ADGM office in September. Gulf LPs increasingly want partnership and co-invest, not just a fund ticket.
