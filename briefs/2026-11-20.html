Launch

Balyasny is telling investors it is closed to new capital

The $37bn multistrategy firm, which has nearly doubled from $20bn at end-2024, won’t take new money for the foreseeable future, per one person familiar; the firm declined to comment. It is down 2.7% for 2026 through October. BI notes a Goldman report from September that more than a third of the 100 largest firms have closed flagships, while big pensions and Gulf and Singapore sovereigns plan to add tens of billions to hedge funds. More capacity-constrained platforms means more allocator demand spilling over to mid-sized and emerging managers. Single source.

Gulf

A Houthi missile damaged PIF’s Riyadh headquarters

The 80-storey PIF Tower in King Abdullah Financial District had windows blown out across seven to eight floors on Thursday; Saudi civil defence reported no injuries and KAFD was closed to visitors. It is the second hit on a Riyadh financial district landmark in under two weeks, after Aramco’s building on 8 November. Expect travel and due diligence questions from managers with Riyadh meetings or offices, and keep an eye on any change in PIF’s pace of commitments.

Abu Dhabi pitched New York: 26 financial firms with about $6tn plan ADGM presences this year

At the second New York Abu Dhabi Investment Forum, ADIO’s director general said more than half of the 26 institutions announcing ADGM plans in 2026 are American, ADGM assets under management grew 54% year on year in H1, and the UAE has more than $1tn invested in the US. More US managers on the ground in Abu Dhabi means more competition for the same sovereign meetings.
