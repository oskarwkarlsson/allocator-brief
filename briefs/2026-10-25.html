Hedge fund assets hit a record $5.64tn; CalPERS weighs a return via SMAs; firms running $5.8tn open first Gulf offices

Top stories
Perf

HFR: industry assets edge to a record $5.64tn in Q3

Net inflows of $34.5bn beat $27.4bn of performance losses, a 16th straight quarter of growth. Big funds won again (asset-weighted +1.2% vs fund-weighted -0.6%) and CTAs led at +5.7%, so expect allocators to keep favouring scale and macro in Q4 pitches.

Hedgeweek, citing HFR →
LPs

CalPERS explores a hedge fund return, 12 years after quitting

Public records show talks with Goldman Sachs and Dockside Platforms about getting exposure through separately managed accounts, led by deputy CIO June Kim. No decision yet, but a $654bn pension re-entering would be a signal other US publics follow, and it would come via SMAs, not commingled funds.

Hedgeweek, citing P&I →
Gulf

Firms running $5.8tn have opened first Gulf offices since the Iran war began

Semafor counts nearly 20 firms overseeing more than $5tn committing to Abu Dhabi since Feb 28, versus 10 managers with $670bn in Dubai. More competition for the same sovereign tickets, and more local LP-coverage hires (Oct 22).

Semafor →
Talent

Multi-manager hiring slows as platforms push money to outside managers

Goldman says platform headcount grew 7% in the year to mid-2026 (27% in 2023), while external AUM rose from $34bn to $47bn and average tickets grew 19% to about $190m. That is a real seeding channel for emerging managers.
