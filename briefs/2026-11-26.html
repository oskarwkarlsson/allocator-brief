LaunchLaunches, raises & closures

New funds, capital raised, fund closes, wind-downs and fundraising halts.

  • Citadel’s give-back extends its habit: about $33bn returned from 2017 to 2025, with D.E. Shaw and Point72 doing the same in recent years (Bloomberg via TBS). Allocators chasing the big names will keep finding the door shut.
  • From 19 Nov: CFM filed a Form D for CFM V-Series Fund, a new open-ended 3(c)(7) vehicle with $250.25m from two investors and a $100m minimum (FormDSignal, from EDGAR). Looks like a large-ticket institutional sleeve.
  • Ex-Blackstone (BAAM) fund structurer Dana Auslander, who raised a $1bn credit fund at Harbinger, now builds Hermes bag funds for family offices through her firm Luxus (Opalesque New Managers).

LPsAllocators & mandates

Pensions, endowments, insurers, SWFs, family offices and FoFs: commitments, results, surveys and policy shifts.

  • Japan’s $2tn GPIF put its basic portfolio back on the November board agenda, and JPMorgan reads that as a step towards an allocation review as early as H1 2027, possibly raising its 25% JGB target (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • PensionDanmark (DKK385bn) names Erik Bennike CIO from January 2027, succeeding Claus Stampe; he already runs equities and credit, about 55% of assets, so expect continuity on private markets and alternatives (Hedgeweek).
  • Fidelity’s endowment and foundation survey finds nonprofits weighing higher spending even as more of them expect a funding shortfall (Institutional Investor). Payout pressure favours liquid, diversifying sleeves.
  • At AsianInvestor’s Southeast Asia forum, JPMorgan AM’s Charles Wu said asset owners are rethinking alternatives with regulated utilities, energy charters and hedge fund alpha as the top themes (AsianInvestor).

GulfGulf capital & Abu Dhabi

ADIA, Mubadala, ADQ/L'IMAD, PIF, QIA and peers, plus managers setting up in ADGM and DIFC.

  • CIBC plans a permanent capital markets office in ADGM, subject to approvals, explicitly to deepen ties with sovereign wealth funds, family offices and institutions across the GCC (ADGM / CIBC, 24 Nov).
  • China Securities International (Citic group, about $75bn AUM) opened its first overseas office in DIFC to channel two-way China and Gulf investment (The National).
  • Saudi Arabia’s 2027 budget cuts spending 3%, including subsidies, health and education, while debt-financing costs rise (AGBI). Tighter state finances are the backdrop to PIF’s first rating.
  • The National argues Trump’s threat against AI data-centre deals could turn into an opportunity for the Gulf (The National, opinion/analysis).

PerfPerformance & flows

Index and peer returns, notable fund numbers, redemptions and prime-book positioning.

  • Goldman’s Vincent Lin: clients cut exposure five weeks running, then piled into shorts, with ETF shorts up about 19 points in a week, the third-largest jump in a decade; fundamental long/short went from about +17% at midyear to about +7% (investingLive, Goldman Sachs video).
  • Goldman’s desk expects CTAs to sell about $68bn of global equities over five days, and vol-control funds have sold about $400bn since mid-October (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • Arini is selling part of its iTraxx Crossover first-loss tranche, with Sona and Polus among the buyers, as the position comes under pressure (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • Baupost made 3% to 4% across its entities through Q3, per Seth Klarman’s letter (Hedgeweek, citing P&I).

TalentPlatforms, PM moves & seeding

Multi-manager hiring, pod builds, spinouts, seed and acceleration deals.

  • Baupost loses two partners at year-end: Andy McElaney, co-head of public equity, leaves after eight years, and James David after 17; Michael Sperling becomes sole head of public equity (Hedgeweek, citing P&I).
  • At Rokos, interim co-CEO Jason Sippel, who joined last year, will oversee investments, quant and technology, while Nifadopoulos runs the rest of the business (New Money via iNewsGR, citing Bloomberg).

PeopleIR, capital raising & BD moves

Hires and exits in investor relations, capital formation, client and consultant relations.

  • Rokos’ Sebag-Montefiore joined in 2018 as head of strategy and became CEO in 2024 after Mark Edwards; the investor update gave no reason for the exit (AlphaMaven, summarising Bloomberg).
  • From 24 Nov: CIFC ($48bn alternative credit) hired Dan Leavy, ex-Haven Green third-party distribution and former Ireland rugby international, as VP Investor Solutions in London, following Nick White’s arrival as MD, European marketing and IR (CIFC).

TermsFees, terms & structures

Fee levels, pass-through, liquidity terms, share classes, evergreen and semi-liquid wrappers.

  • AQR’s new Delphi Plus share class carries a 25% performance fee, above its previous 20% maximum; JPMorgan AM and AllianceBernstein are building tax-efficient offerings too (Hedgeweek, citing Bloomberg).
  • CNBC on the “retailization” of hedge funds: hedge-fund-like ETFs typically charge a single 0.4% to 1.25% fee, and the wrapper, not the strategy, is what’s changing (CNBC).
  • CAIA’s Nick Pollard: evergreen assets have doubled since 2022, driven by wealth investors, with Asia leading (CAIA).

RulesMarketing, ops & compliance

AIFMD II, SEC marketing rule, Form PF, FINRA, FCA, ADGM FSRA/DFSA and enforcement touching how funds market.

  • IRS Notice 2026-62 describes several tax-aware fund techniques (identified straddles, selectively elected currency forwards, selective swap terminations) as potentially abusive and warns future guidance could be retroactive (HedgeCo).
  • Deadlines coming up: comments on the SEC’s plan to let any accredited investor pay performance fees (Rule 205-3) are due 7 Dec, and on the new accredited-investor routes, including a FINRA exam, on 4 Dec (Troutman Pepper Locke). Subscription docs and investor screens would simplify if it passes.
