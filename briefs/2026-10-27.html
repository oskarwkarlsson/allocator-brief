Gulf

Apollo takes a minority stake in Mubadala Capital and commits capital to its strategies

Other strategic investors will also join as minority holders, with an expected $5bn committed to Mubadala Capital's strategies, while Mubadala stays majority owner and the firm keeps independent governance. The firm runs over $60bn across five strategies. Bloomberg puts Apollo's stake at nearly 10%. For managers pitching Abu Dhabi, Mubadala's own alts arm is now raising third-party money alongside a $1tn partner.

Citco: $36bn of Q3 net inflows despite a -0.7% weighted return

Year-to-date inflows are now well over $100bn. Multi-strategy took $15.4bn, hybrid $12.3bn and funds of funds $5.7bn, and funds over $10bn took $24.9bn. Commodities (+11.9%) and macro (+3.1%) led the quarter, event driven (-3.8%) lagged. A second administrator now confirms money is concentrating in the biggest platforms.

Meiji Yasuda lifts its Man Group commitment to ¥200bn and may buy about 5% of Man

The roughly $1.25bn goes mainly to Man's private credit over three years, with co-developed products for Japan. Any stake comes through open-market purchases, with a three-year lock-up and a 19.99% standstill. Japanese life insurers pairing big tickets with equity stakes is a model worth knowing for anyone covering Asian insurers.

Verition is sued by its former APAC business development head

Elizabeth Xiang (Xiang Liyao) asked Hong Kong's High Court to find her dismissal for underperformance unfair and seeks unspecified damages, per a statement of claim dated Oct 20. Her role was finding and recruiting PMs, so this is a rare BD-side dispute at a $15bn multi-manager.
