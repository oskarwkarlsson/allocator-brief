Xantium plans a record ~$9bn spinout from Tudor

The biggest hedge fund spinout on record (Systematica took $8.4bn from BlueCrest in 2015), with offices in London, New York, Salt Lake City and Abu Dhabi and mostly external money. The FT notes new managers now need hundreds of millions lined up before launch, so this resets the bar for day-one asks.

Mubadala signs up OpenAI for investment analysis

Mubadala, G42's Inception42 and OpenAI agreed to apply AI to investment analysis, opportunity identification and risk assessment, one of four Abu Dhabi deals signed Monday. Expect more AI-driven screening on the allocator side of the table in Abu Dhabi.

Hedge funds have unwound every AI stock bought since early 2025

Goldman prime data show the AI buying since the start of 2025 fully reversed, and Morgan Stanley says funds cut more than 8% of stock bets since June, the biggest retreat since at least 2017. October letters will need a clear de-risking story.
