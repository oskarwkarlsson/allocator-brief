ADIA keeps its 10% of Sempra Infrastructure as a KKR-led group closes its $10bn deal

An SEC filing shows ADIA held on to its stake and its board seat after Sempra sold 45% of the business to KKR affiliates and CPP Investments. The deal implies about $22bn of equity value, putting ADIA's stake near $2.2bn, about 28% above the $1.73bn it paid in 2022. A reminder of how ADIA likes to work: as a long-term partner alongside managers it knows, not only as an LP, so co-investment capacity belongs in any ADIA pitch.

iConnections Global Alts Asia opens today at Marina Bay Sands

Three days, 16 to 18 November, with about 400 LPs, 300 GPs and 5,000+ pre-scheduled meetings, per the organiser. Single family offices are 44% of allocators and sovereign or government agencies 7%; 40% of attending managers run hedge fund strategies. ADIC's Mark Nicholas Cutis, a former ADGM CEO, sits on the LP advisory board, alongside Khazanah, APG Asia and Albourne. The first big Asian cap intro week since October's losses, so expect drawdown questions in every room.

Anthropic prices on Wednesday with a book about six times covered

Reuters reported the roughly $64bn deal was six times oversubscribed by Thursday, and later reports say Goldman and Morgan Stanley are weighing pricing above the $70 to $78 range, which could value Anthropic above $1.5tn. Sovereign funds and pensions that hold pre-IPO stakes will get a public mark, and a deal this size soaks up allocator cash this week. Expect LPs to ask how much of your book sits in the AI complex. Pricing reports rest on unnamed sources.

Perf

HedgeCo: the de-gross is a buying moment only for allocators who want diversification

Saturday's piece lines up the week's data: Morgan Stanley's prime desk saw long/short equity gross down 10.7% since late June while multi-strategy, macro and quant books covered shorts, and Renaissance's Institutional Equities fund made 6% in October as Rokos lost 4.9%. Its read is that CPPIB and Colorado FPPA are buying engines that diversify, not broad hedge fund beta. A useful frame for any manager pitching right now: show where your returns come from and how they differ from the crowded book.
