Millennium raised $22bn of callable capital with a five-year lock-up

BI's profile of president and COO Ajay Nagpal says investors in the latest raise, led by Nagpal and longtime CMO John Novogratz, committed money Millennium can call at any time over three years, then locked for five years once called, per an allocator. Only about 5% of the firm's capital can now be redeemed in a quarter, against roughly 25% a decade ago. The $95bn firm lost money in Q3 and was down 1.8% in October. This is the new benchmark for what the strongest platforms can ask for, and every allocator conversation about liquidity terms now starts here. Millennium declined to comment.

Connecticut swaps its tail-risk hedge funds for return-seeking managers

The roughly $70bn Connecticut Retirement Plans & Trust Funds cut its absolute-return target to 2% from 5% (actual 1.9%), after the book returned 1.7% a year over one year against a 4.04% benchmark. CIO Ted Wright called risk mitigation "the wrong strategy at the wrong time". Staff dropped Investcorp-Tages' Paladin Defensive Strategy and One River's long-vol fund, and propose adding BlueBay EM Credit Alpha, Diametric True Alpha and DCM Diversified Alpha (about $112m to $126m each), then Ellington Enhanced Income and Lazard Rathmore Plus. A clear signal that pensions want low-beta alpha, not insurance.

Abu Dhabi's MGX sits on about $20bn of unrealised Anthropic gains

An MGX representative told The National its stake carried a roughly $20bn mark-to-market gain at Thursday's $97.17 close; MGX co-led the $30bn February round, joined the $65bn Series H and added at the IPO. ADIC and QIA were also major backers. Shares then fell about 8.5% to $88.9 on Friday. MGX plans to put up to 80% of its portfolio into US projects. Abu Dhabi's AI wins strengthen the hand of its investment teams in setting priorities, which matters for managers pitching AI-adjacent strategies to the emirate.

Family offices cut hedge funds to 7.5% of portfolios

Addepar's anonymised data on hundreds of single-family offices with more than $1.4tn in assets shows hedge fund allocations down to 7.5% in Q3 from 8.9% a year earlier, while public equities rose to 36.4% and cash to 8.6%. Alternatives overall fell to 47% from 48.5%. Family offices are a growth channel many IR teams are leaning on, so this is a useful reality check before year-end meetings. Single data source.
