Viking tells LPs its long-short spread, its “Holy Grail” KPI, has been below expectations

In a 15 October letter seen by Bloomberg, Andreas Halvorsen said the $35bn firm's portfolio had been “too balanced”: the Q3 spread was just 0.3% as the flagship fell 0.3% against 2% for MSCI World, and it is up about 1% for the year after losing 1.2% in October. Viking is pushing analysts and PMs to bet with more conviction and has adjusted incentives to reward them when those bets work. A model of naming your own KPI and owning the miss in an LP letter; expect allocators to ask other long-short managers for their spread. Single source (Bloomberg).

Bobby Jain: AI and compute will drive hedge fund consolidation

On Nishant Kumar's new Bloomberg hedge fund podcast, Jain said Jain Global, which has managed money exclusively for Millennium since September, is “a separate business, with a common investor base” behind information barriers. It has hired 20 PMs since the deal and made 41% gross on called capital in its first two years. He said access to compute, data and AI expertise could take a standalone firm up to five years to build, which is why he expects consolidation. That is the pitch emerging managers now have to answer when LPs ask why not a platform.

SS&C GlobeOp: funds lost 2.18% gross in October but net inflows kept coming

The administrator's index is still up 5.73% for the year and 8.31% over 12 months, and its Capital Movement Index rose 0.52% in November to 133.32, a new 12-month high (still below the 150.77 peak of 2013). GlobeOp covers about 10% of industry assets. A useful counterweight in LP meetings to a week of headlines about deleveraging and pod cuts. Next release 11 December.

FT's Marc Rubinstein: recent hedge fund stumbles are a hot-money problem

Saturday's column argues the recent blow-ups came from chasing heady growth without the risk management and structures to handle it. It lands after Arini, Walleye and Millennium's capital pulls, and gives allocators a frame for asking how fast a manager has grown versus its risk team. Worth having an answer on capacity, growth pace and investor concentration ready. Paywalled; only the headline and standfirst were readable.
