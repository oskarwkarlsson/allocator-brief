Positioning

Hedge fund short positioning is near a five-year high as the momentum trade fades

Goldman's prime brokerage October report shows managers leaning into shorts while Big Tech shed nearly $900bn. Bayberry founder Angela Aldrich is reportedly adding a short-only strategy (single source, BI). Expect allocators to ask how your net and gross moved this month, so have the numbers ready.

LPs

Davidson Kempner moves into UK pension superfunds through its Genwood unit

Former Standard Life executives Tom Ground and Kunal Sood join as directors. The UK defined-benefit market is worth more than £1tn, and the platform has not yet been assessed by the regulator. A hedge fund firm buying pension liabilities outright is a new way to reach pension capital (Bloomberg).

Gulf

ADGM's regulator granted six licence approvals in October as eight global managers arrived

Capital Group got its permission, and Eurazeo opened its first Middle East office led by an investor relations managing director. Advent's Abu Dhabi team is led by its head of LP services. Firms are sending IR people first, which tells you who the Gulf hires will be. Fund and asset managers in ADGM rose to 190 from 154 a year earlier.

Credit

Arini sold about $800m of debt after margin calls, and Pimco and Oaktree are bidding for more

The roughly $7bn flagship lost more than 20% this year as of mid-October, on distressed telecom bets (WSJ, Bloomberg). Arini has grown into a $22bn manager, so this is a test of how a fast-growing firm talks to LPs through a drawdown.
