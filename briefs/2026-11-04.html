DE Shaw hits a record $125bn with tougher terms as October trips up most big funds; QIA backs a £20bn AI campus at Drax

Top stories
Terms

DE Shaw hits a record $125bn and adds a tech surcharge

Assets are up from $98bn in January, and it raised about $6bn for Cogence, its first discretionary fund, with lockups of up to four years. Composite now carries a 0.12% technology surcharge on top of 3 and 35. Proof that top performers can still push terms, and a reference point when LPs ask what “expensive” means.

Perf

October was a broad down month, Citadel the standout

Citadel Wellington gained 1.7% (15.3% YTD) while Millennium lost 2.3%, ExodusPoint 2.4% and Marshall Wace Eureka 5.4%. Goldman's prime desk says it was the first month on record with fundamental long/short and systematic both down more than 3%. Expect allocators to ask hard questions about drawdown control in year-end reviews.

QIA and Brookfield back a £20bn AI data centre at Drax

The Brookfield and Qai joint venture puts up an initial £2.5bn for a 100MW first phase, aiming for more than 1GW at the North Yorkshire site. Another sign Gulf sovereign capital is going into AI infrastructure through co-investment platforms rather than fund commitments.

Global managers crowd into Abu Dhabi for secondaries

EQT, Pantheon and Eurazeo have opened offices since mid-September, joining Ardian and HarbourVest. ADIA reportedly pledged $2bn to Ardian's $30bn secondaries platform, and Adic expects to deploy up to $10bn over three years. Local presence is becoming table stakes, but sovereigns are also competing for deals.
