Nvidia beats and guides to $130bn, the same day Muddy Waters discloses a short

Q3 revenue was $115.8bn against $109.15bn expected, data centre revenue $107.6bn (+110% y/y), and Q4 guidance about $130bn versus $125.5bn; the board said it will not reinstate Jensen Huang and has hired Heidrick & Struggles. Hours earlier Muddy Waters published a short asking whether a woman photographed with former EVP Ajay Puri is the ex-Super Micro executive named in a class action over GPU sales to China; it states nobody by that name has been accused by any government. Every tech-heavy book will be asked about this week in December calls.

Australia lets super funds test up to 5% of portfolios against a CPI-plus benchmark

Treasurer Jim Chalmers told the ASFA conference in Melbourne on Wednesday that a voluntary “emerging assets” benchmark (CPI plus a margin set on Government Actuary advice) will apply to new investments from the 2028–29 test, which the government says could unlock up to A$90bn. The test also extends to externally directed products, adding about 1.4 million accounts. Benchmark hugging has kept Australia's giant super funds away from anything index-unlike; this loosens that for infrastructure, venture and private assets.

Former Citadel Surveyor PM George Chen targets $1bn for healthcare fund Breitenfeld

Chen, at Surveyor from 2017 to August 2025, is launching a market-neutral healthcare hedge fund and hopes to raise $1bn, with trading planned for Q2 2027. Day-one team: Echo Street's Andrew Yang and Millennium's Leo Hei as investment partners, and Brian Kessler as COO. A pod alum taking the market-neutral playbook independent in a year when allocators are pruning rosters but still writing tickets to smaller managers.

Gulf

JPMorgan upgrades MSCI UAE to overweight, saying foreign inflows have returned

In a Tuesday client note JPMorgan said UAE “earnings revisions have swung positive, valuations have upside and foreign inflows have returned”, and that the current “no war/no peace” state is good enough for the market to outperform CEEMEA. A useful third-party line for Gulf-focused strategies and for anyone fielding questions on UAE risk before ADFW in December.
