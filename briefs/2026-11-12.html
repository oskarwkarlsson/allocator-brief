Clear Street and Marex prepare prime-broker access to Kalshi for hedge funds

Clear Street expects to start clearing Kalshi trades in late March and Marex plans access in the months after; Marex says large hedge funds approached it directly, and it cleared its first institutional Kalshi trade on 3 November. State gambling challenges and CFTC versus SEC jurisdiction remain the hurdle. Once event contracts sit inside existing PB relationships, expect macro and event-driven managers to pitch them as hedges, and allocators to ask how they are sized and valued. Bloomberg reporting, via Hedgeweek and HedgeCo.

PIF explores securitisations and stake sales to fund growth without new equity

The $900bn-plus fund is weighing securitising cash flows from portfolio companies, selling stakes in mature assets, external money for younger firms, bonds and export-credit debt, three sources told Reuters. Talks are early, with valuation and control disputes slowing stake sales; PIF's annualised shareholder return since 2017 fell to 5.8% at end-2025 from 7.2%. A PIF that recycles rather than injects capital means fewer new mandates and more co-investment and structured-credit asks for managers covering Riyadh.

Goldman, HSBC and Mubadala-backed Fortress join a rare $150m Saudi private credit deal

They lend to United Saudi Company, a nonwoven fabrics maker, alongside regional managers Ruya Partners and Amwal Capital Partners; it is Fortress's first Saudi private credit deal and one of the first big international ones since the war began in February. Ruya, backed by Mubadala and PIF, is raising about $400m and Janus Henderson has raised nearly $200m for a regional private credit fund. Bloomberg reporting, via Yahoo Finance and Enterprise.

Many of the biggest hedge funds are missing from the databases allocators use

Institutional Investor's podcast with Jon Caplis argues that indices built from commercial databases understate hedge funds because large, institutional-quality managers do not report to them. UNC's Institute for Private Capital counted 178 of 649 managers with $1bn-plus in primary hedge fund assets absent from commercial databases. Useful ammunition when an LP benchmarks you against a database index after a weak October.
